Garage Door Questions, Answered

Get clear answers about garage door repairs, replacements, installations, openers, and more. If you do not see your question here, contact our team for straightforward help.

Why will my garage door not open or close?

The issue may be a broken spring, malfunctioning opener, misaligned safety sensors, damaged track, or worn hardware. A professional inspection can identify the cause and recommend the right repair.

What should I do if my garage door comes off track?

Stop using the door immediately. Continuing to run the opener can cause more damage to the tracks, rollers, cables, and panels. A technician can safely reset the door, inspect the system, and repair the underlying cause..

How long does a garage door installation take

Most standard residential garage door installations can be completed in one visit once the selected door and required materials are available. Installation time can vary based on the door style, opener setup, removal of the existing door, and any needed repairs to the framing or tracks.

Do you install new garage door openers?

Yes. We install garage door openers along with remotes, wireless keypads, safety sensors, and other compatible accessories. We can help you select an opener that fits your garage door, usage needs, and budget.
